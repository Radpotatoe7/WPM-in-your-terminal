The world of Nier Automata is quiet and ruined. Humans fled to the moon long ago after machines invaded Earth. The androids known as YoRHa fight an endless proxy war to reclaim the planet. Two android soldiers named 2B and 9S explore city ruins, sunken factories, and vast sand dunes. They soon discover that the enemy machines have developed feelings, families, and broken philosophical ideals. Every battle makes the purpose of the war feel distant and strange, yet they continue to fight because duty is all they know.
In Persona 3 Reload, a high school transfer student arrives on an artificial island called Tatsumi Port Island. When midnight strikes, the regular flow of time freezes, and a hidden period known as the Dark Hour begins. Most humans turn into resting coffins during this hour, while monstrous Shadows roam the streets. A group of students awakens their inner Personas to climb the massive tower of Tartarus. They face mortality, form deep bonds with friends, and accept that death gives meaning to life.
Far away in the kingdom of Lothric, the fire is slowly fading. Dark Souls 3 tells the story of an Ashen One, an undead warrior who was not even fit to become kindling. The great Lords of Cinder have abandoned their thrones, refusing to burn themselves to sustain the age of fire. You must travel through high walls, poison swamps, and decayed cathedrals to bring their ashes back. Every knight, hollow, and monster in this ruined realm fights with desperate fury.
The androids look down at rusted skyscrapers covered in green moss. The machine cores pulse with energy, mimicking human rituals of dance, prayer, and grief. Pod units float nearby to offer tactical reports, but they cannot explain the sorrow of existence. The war continues because victory is a myth created to give synthetic souls a reason to keep moving forward.
At the center of Tartarus, shadows gather around strange green light. The students summon their powers with specialized evokers, braving exhaustion and cold fear. As the moon waxes full each month, stronger shadows emerge to threaten the city. The bonds forged over tea, athletics, and study sessions become the very strength needed to face the end of the world.
In the cold ash of Lothric, an old bell rings across the empty cemetery. The Fire Keeper stands silently beside a small bonfire, waiting for someone to guide the embers. The cycle of flame and shadow has repeated for ages, turning kings into mad beasts and castles into dust. An Ashen One picks up a rusted sword, resting briefly at the warm light before stepping into the fog.
